# Privacy Policy — Tomato Focus

**Last updated: 6 September 2026**

Tomato Focus is a Pomodoro timer and habit tracker. This policy explains what
the app does and does not do with your information. It is short because the
app does very little.

## The short version

Tomato Focus does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. There are
no accounts, no servers, no analytics, and no advertising. Everything you enter
stays on your device.

## What the app stores, and where

All of the following is stored **only in the app's private storage on your
device**:

- **Focus session history** — when each session started and ended, how long it
  ran, which preset it used, and whether it completed.
- **Habits** — the names, icons, colours and schedules you create, and the days
  you marked them done.
- **Settings** — your theme choice, timer presets, alarm tone, and volume and
  vibration preferences.

None of this is transmitted anywhere. The app has no internet permission and
makes no network requests of any kind.

## What the app does not collect

The app does not collect or access your name, email address, phone number,
contacts, location, photos, camera, microphone, files, calendar, health data,
device identifiers, advertising ID, or usage analytics.

## Permissions

The app requests a small number of Android permissions, all in service of the
timer:

- **Notifications** — to alert you when a focus session or break ends.
- **Alarms & reminders (exact alarms)** — so that alert arrives at the exact
  second rather than minutes late.
- **Foreground service** — to keep the timer counting and show its live
  countdown while the app is not in the foreground.
- **Keep screen awake** — only while the focus display is open, and only if you
  have enabled that option.
- **Vibrate** — for the completion alert, which you can turn off.
- **Run at startup** — so a pending completion alert survives a device restart.
- **Ignore battery optimisations** — optional, requested only if you choose it
  from Settings, for devices whose power saving would otherwise stop the timer.

The app requests no internet, location, contacts, camera, storage, or account
permissions.

## Sharing

Nothing is shared with anyone. There are no third-party SDKs, advertising
networks, or analytics providers in the app.

If you use **Settings → Data → Export my data**, the app writes a JSON file
containing your habits and session history and hands it to your device's share
sheet. Where that file goes is entirely your choice, and the app plays no part
in it after that point.

## Retention and deletion

Your data stays on your device until you delete it. You can remove it at any
time by:

- **Settings → Data → Delete all data**, which permanently deletes every habit,
  streak and session; or
- **uninstalling the app**, which removes all of its stored data.

Because there is no server copy, deletion is immediate and irreversible. We
cannot recover it for you.

## Children

Tomato Focus is a general-audience productivity app. It is not directed at
children, and it collects no data from anyone, including children.

## Security

Your data is held in the app's private, sandboxed storage, which other apps on
your device cannot read. Since nothing is transmitted, there is no data in
transit to intercept.

## Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app changes what it collects, this policy will be
updated before that version is released, and the "last updated" date above will
change. The corresponding Data safety declaration on Google Play will be
updated at the same time.
